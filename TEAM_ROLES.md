# Project Team and Responsibilities

This repository is organized as a four-member group project. The labels below are placeholders; add the actual names to the final report when the team is confirmed.

| Team role | Primary responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Team Leader | Project coordination, architecture, integration, and final review |
| Member 1 | Historical data collection and preprocessing |
| Member 2 | Feature engineering, model training, and prediction pipeline |
| Member 3 | Dashboard, evaluation, testing, and documentation |

All members should participate in integration, testing, review, and final documentation. Responsibilities are an initial allocation and can be adjusted to reflect the work actually completed.
